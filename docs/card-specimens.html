Card specimens

Every face the game can draw, rendered by the same cardFace() and dropFace() the board uses, under the same stylesheet. Two independent axes: the family decides the frame and the ground, the rarity decides the star badge and the halo. The three families are ranked by how much the player wants them: a clue is a photograph, a collection card is gilt art, and a Collectible — what three copies of a card become — is a number on a plaque. One card wears two of those faces. Written up in CARDS.md.
